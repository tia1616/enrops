# Run the term

Two weeks out, make sure families know what's coming and your school knows who's coming.

## Turn on your automatic emails {#automations}

**Goal:** families get the right email at the right time without you sending it.

**Steps**
1. **Comms → Automations.**
2. Read each email and tap **Turn on** for the ones you want.

**What each one does**
- **Thank-you:** sent when a registration is paid. Always on once Stripe is connected.
- **Welcome (after-school or camp):** sent before the first class. Off until you turn it on.
- **No-school day heads-up:** reminds families when class doesn't meet. Off until you turn it on.
- **New class photos:** tells families when photos are posted. On.
- **Payment coming up:** reminds families on a payment plan before a payment. On.

**Ennie's advice**
- The welcome email is off until you turn it on.
- Read each email before you turn it on. You're the one families will reply to.
- If your families register on your own website instead of enrops, these emails don't send.

## Your rosters {#rosters}

**Steps**
1. **Programs → Class rosters.** Use **Search school or class** to find one.
2. **View / print →** opens a class. From there you can **Download CSV**, **Print**, or **Email roster to partner**.
3. To add a child yourself: **Add / upload → Add one by hand**. Only the first name is required. Tap **Add to roster**.
4. To upload a list: **Add / upload → Upload a file** (.csv or .xlsx). Check the rows, then **Import N students**.
5. To fix a child's details: expand the class and tap **View / Edit →**.

**Ennie's advice**
- When you add a child by hand, the photo release box starts ticked. Untick it if the family hasn't agreed, and the child shows as pending until they do.
- **Remove** only appears for families who haven't paid, and it can't be undone. For a family who paid, use **Refund / remove** (see **Money moments**).
- If a family wants a different day or class, don't remove them. Use **Move** (next section).

## Move a child to another class {#move}

**Goal:** a child switches class or camp week in one step, and their payment stays as it is.

**Steps**
1. **Programs → Class rosters.** Expand the class the child is in now.
2. Tap **Move** on their row.
3. Under **Move to**, pick the new class. Use **Search by class, school or term** to find it. Each class shows its spots left and its price.
4. Leave **Email the family about the move** ticked, or untick it to tell them yourself.
5. Read the sentence above the button. It names the child, both classes, and what happens to the money.
6. Tap **Move** and the child's name. When it says **Moved.**, tap **Done**.

**Ennie's advice**
- No money moves. If the new class costs a different amount, the sentence shows both prices so you can sort out the difference yourself (a partial refund or a note to the family).
- The email names the new class, where and when it meets, and the next date. Both parents on file get it. The new class's usual welcome email still goes out if that class hasn't started.
- If the new class is full, you're warned first. The button changes to **Move ... anyway**, and the class goes over its seat count.
- A class moves to a class and a camp to a camp. A class that has already finished isn't in the list.
- If the child was on the new class's waiting list, that place clears on its own. Their old seat is offered to the old class's waiting list.
- For one term of a year-long sign-up, only that term moves. Their other terms stay where they are.
- The child's instructor sees a short note on their roster: "Moved here from ... on ...".

## Send the roster to your school partner {#roster-to-partner}

**Steps**
1. **Programs → Class rosters → View / print →** on the class.
2. Tap **Email roster to partner**.
3. If the site isn't linked to a school partner yet, you're asked to pick one. If none are listed, go to **Programs → Locations**, open the site, and tap **Add as a school partner**.
4. Tick the contacts who should get it, or type other emails separated by commas. To add the school's contacts: **Programs → Locations**, **Open** the school, then **Contacts → + Add contact**.
5. Tap **Send roster**. The roster is attached as a PDF, and the subject is filled in from the class details.

**Ennie's advice**
- Send it before class one, and again if anyone joins late.

## Invite families to the parent portal {#invite-families}

**Goal:** families can sign in, see class details, and update their own pickup information.

**Steps**
1. **Programs → Class rosters → Invite families →** on the class.
2. Check the preview. It shows how many families will get it and what the email says.
3. Tap **Send N invites**.

**Ennie's advice**
- Families who already signed in, or have no email on file, are skipped, so it's safe to send again.
- Families don't need an invite. They can always go to your page, tap sign in, and get a sign-in link by email.
- In the portal, families sign your required waivers before they see class details.

## Instructors {#instructors}

Instructor tools (onboarding, schedule, subs, attendance and pay) are part of the founding and enterprise plans. If **Instructors** isn't in your menu, they aren't on your plan yet. Email hello@enrops.com if you'd like to use them.

When you do have them:
- **Instructors → Instructor Roster → + Add instructor**, then **Send onboarding invite**.
- Instructors sign in at your page address plus `/instructor`.
- Putting an instructor on a class doesn't email them. Send the offer when you're ready.
- Instructors take attendance and record who picked each child up in their portal. You read it in **Programs → Class Reports**.
