# Fill your classes

## Bring in the families you already know {#import-families}

**Goal:** your existing family list lives in enrops, so you can email them about classes.

**What to have ready:** a spreadsheet (.csv, .xlsx or .xls), or a PDF or Word document. Email is the only column you must have. Parent name, phone, child's name, school and birthdate all help.

**Steps**
1. **Comms → Contacts → Upload contacts →**.
2. **Choose your file**, then **Read file →**.
3. Match your columns to ours. Only **Email** is required.
4. Optional: tag everyone in this upload, for example "Lincoln families 2025".
5. Choose **New families** (they get your welcome email if it's on) or **My existing families** (no welcome, for people who already know you).
6. Tick **I have permission to email these contacts.** and tap **Add N contacts**.

**Ennie's advice**
- A PDF or Word list works too. We read it for you, then you check the columns before anything is saved.
- Only upload families who agreed to hear from you. The upload asks you to confirm that.

## Email the families in a class {#message-families}

**Goal:** send one email to everyone in one or more classes.

**Steps**
1. **Programs → Class rosters.**
2. Tick the classes, then tap **Message families (N classes)**.
3. Optional: include families on the waiting list, or families who left or were refunded.
4. Write a **Subject** and your message. Use **Send a test to** to check it first, or tick **Email me a copy**.
5. Tap **Send to N families**. Sent emails are listed on the **Sent** tab.

**Ennie's advice**
- The count is families, not children. Two siblings in one class get one email.
- Send yourself a test first. It's the fastest way to catch a typo or a broken link.

## Sibling discounts and promo codes {#discounts}

**Steps (sibling discount)**
1. **Money → Discounts.**
2. Under the sibling discount, type a percent, for example 10. That's taken off each additional child in the same order. Leave it blank to turn it off.
3. Tap **Save**.

**Steps (promo code)**
1. **Money → Discounts → + New code.**
2. Type the **Code**, choose **% off** or **$ off**, and set **Starts** and **Ends** dates.
3. Optional: limit **Total uses**, uses **Per family**, a minimum order (**Min. cart**), or which programs it **Applies to**.
4. Tap **Create code**.

**Ennie's advice**
- A family with two children is deciding whether to sign up twice. A small automatic discount usually costs you less than losing that second registration.
- Give each code a reason and an end date. Open-ended codes get passed around and quietly become your new price.
- A 100% code means families pay nothing. Use it for a scholarship or a make-up class.
- Codes apply on top of sibling savings.

## Families who started but didn't finish {#unfinished-registrations}

- A roster shows "(+N unfinished registrations, not counted)" when a family started registering but didn't pay. They aren't enrolled yet and haven't paid.
- Automatic follow-up emails to these families aren't on the free plan. Email hello@enrops.com if you need it.
