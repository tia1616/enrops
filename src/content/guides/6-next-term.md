# Next term

## Copy your classes to the next term {#copy-to-term}

**Steps**
1. **Programs → Scheduled programs.** Make sure the term picker shows the term you're copying from.
2. Tap **Copy classes to another term**. Tick the classes, choose the term to copy them into, and copy. Camps aren't included here, so copy each camp on its own. (For one class or camp: expand it, and under **Copy to another term** pick the season and year, then tap **Copy to (term) →**.)
3. Switch the term picker to the new term to find the copies. They're drafts.
4. Expand each copy and set its **First class date**. A copy can't be published until it has one.
5. Check the price and times, then **Publish →**.

**Ennie's advice**
- Copies come over without dates on purpose, so you can't publish last term's dates by accident.
- Before you publish, check the school calendar for the new term is saved (see **Make your dates skip no-school days**).

## Open the new term to families {#open-term}

- When a term isn't open yet, Programs shows "(term) is not open for registration." with **Open (term) for registration →**.
- Opening a term switches what families see. Your page shows that term's weekly classes, and stops showing the old term's. Families already enrolled aren't affected.
- Camps stay on your page until their last day, whatever the term.

**Ennie's advice**
- When the new term is open, tell this term's families with **Message families**.

## What the term codes mean {#term-codes}

- A term code is a season plus a two-digit year: **FA** fall, **WI** winter, **SP** spring, **SU** summer. **WI27** is winter 2027.
