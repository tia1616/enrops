# Money moments

## How you get paid {#how-you-get-paid}

- A family pays. The money goes into your own Stripe account.
- Stripe pays it out to your bank. On Stripe's standard US schedule that's about 2 business days after the payment. Bank-transfer payments take 1 to 3 business days to clear first. Your first payout can take longer while Stripe reviews a new account.
- **Money → Payments** shows the class fees that came in, refunds, and what's expected soon.
- Your actual bank deposits are in your Stripe dashboard. Tap **Open Stripe →** or **Stripe Dashboard ↗**.

## What families pay, and what enrops takes {#fees}

- Families pay your price plus the enrops service fee. They see it on its own line before they pay.
- Stripe takes its standard processing fee before the money reaches your bank. That's Stripe's charge, the same as with any card payment tool.
- The price screen shows exactly what families pay: "Families pay $X". Use that number anywhere you advertise.

**Ennie's advice**
- Families see the fee before they pay, on purpose. Costs that appear at the last step are the number one reason people abandon an online order (40%, according to the Baymard Institute). Shown up front, the same cost doesn't do that.
- Call it the "enrops service fee", the same words families see at checkout.

## Refund a family {#refund}

**Steps**
1. **Programs → Class rosters.** Expand the class.
2. Tap **Refund / remove** on the family's row.
3. Choose **What they get**: **Refund to their card**, or **Give enrops credit instead**.
4. For a card refund, choose **Their spot**: **Refund only — keep their spot**, or **Refund and withdraw — free their spot**. For a credit there's no spot choice, because a credit always frees their spot. Instead choose who ended it: **We cancelled the class** or **The family cancelled**.
5. Leave **Full refund**, or type a smaller amount for a partial refund.
6. Add a **Reason**. It's an internal note and isn't sent to the family.
7. Tap **Refund $X** (or **Give $X credit**).

**Ennie's advice**
- The family gets an email receipt for a card refund. A credit or a withdrawal without a refund sends no email, so tell them yourself.
- A refund takes 5 to 10 business days to show on the family's card.
- Before the class starts, the enrops service fee is refunded in full. After it starts, only the share for sessions not yet held comes back. Stripe's processing cost isn't returned when a payment is refunded. That stays with your business, which is standard for card payments.
- Credit is applied automatically the next time that family registers with the same email address. Credit can't be used on a payment plan.
- Money → Payments → **Refunds** lists every refund you've made.

## Cancel a whole class {#cancel-class}

**Steps**
1. **Programs → Scheduled programs.** Expand the class and tap **Cancel class**.
2. Confirm with **Cancel the class**.
3. Cancelling doesn't refund anyone or send any emails. Go to **Programs → Class rosters**, open the class, and refund each family.
4. Tell families. Use **Message families** and tick **Also include families who have left or been refunded**, so the ones you just refunded get it too.

**Ennie's advice**
- When you cancel, families should get their money back. When a family cancels, a credit is often the kinder option for both of you.
- Changed your mind? **Reopen as draft** brings the class back.

## Stop selling a class {#stop-selling}

- A published class keeps taking registrations until you stop it, even after it has started. There's no automatic close date yet.
- To stop it, open the class in **Programs → Scheduled programs** and tap **Unpublish**. It goes back to draft, and families already registered stay registered.
