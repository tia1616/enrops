# Make it yours

We set you up with sensible starting points. This is where you make them yours. Each one takes a few minutes.

## Save your registration questions {#registration-questions}

**Goal:** your form asks exactly what you need to know, including how each child goes home.

**Steps**
1. **Settings → Registration questions → Manage →**.
2. Under **Optional standard questions**, review the pickup and dismissal questions. They start switched on.
3. Under **Ways a family can say their child leaves**, tick every option you actually allow: released to a parent or authorized adult, walks or bikes home, takes the bus, goes to aftercare, something else.
4. Tap **Save standard questions**.
5. To ask something of your own, tap **+ Add question** under **Your own questions**. Pick the **Answer type**, choose whether to ask it on every program or one class, and tap **Add question**.

**Ennie's advice**
- The dismissal and pickup questions show as on, but they're only added to your form when you tap **Save standard questions**. Do this before your first family registers.
- **Takes the bus** and **Something else** start switched off. If any of your kids ride a bus, tick **Takes the bus**, or families can't tell you.
- Your form always asks for the child's name, grade and birth date, allergies and medical notes, an emergency contact, the parent's name, email and phone, and a mailing address. You don't need to add those.
- Pick the answer type carefully. A question that needs words should be **Short text** or **Long text**, not a checkbox.

## Read your waivers and cancellation policy {#waivers}

**Goal:** families agree to wording you're comfortable with.

**Steps**
1. **Settings → Waivers & policies → Manage →**.
2. Under **Waivers families sign**, open each one with **Edit**. You start with three: participation and liability, medical and emergency care (both required), and a photo and media release (families can decline).
3. Under **Policies you publish**, open the **Cancellation & Refund Policy**. Edit it, or leave it as is.

**Ennie's advice**
- We wrote the starter cancellation policy so your page wasn't missing one. These are our words, not yours, and they make promises about refunds. Out of the box it says: a full refund before the first session, a refund for sessions not yet attended once a class has started, no refund after it ends, and a full refund if you cancel. Change anything that isn't how you run it.
- Leave **{{org}}** where it is in a waiver. Families see your business name there.
- The starter wording is a helpful beginning, not legal advice. Have a lawyer read anything that really matters to your business.

## Add your logo and page wording {#branding}

**Goal:** your page and your emails look like your business.

**Steps**
1. **Settings → Branding → Manage →**.
2. **Logo:** tap **Upload logo** (SVG, PNG, JPG or WebP, under 2 MB).
3. **Colors:** after you upload, we suggest colors "Found in your logo". Tap **Use these** if you like them.
4. **Banner** (optional): a wide photo across the top of your registration page, about 3 times wider than it is tall.
5. **Registration page wording:** a **Headline** and a **Line underneath**. Leave them blank to use the default.
6. **Confirmation page wording:** what families see after they pay. You can add a button, like "Visit our shop", with a link.
7. Tap **Save**. Nothing you upload is kept until you save.

**Ennie's advice**
- Your logo appears on your registration pages and in your emails.
- Use the confirmation page to say what happens next: when the first class is, what to bring, and how to reach you.

## Make sure family replies reach you {#email-sender}

**Goal:** when a family hits reply, it lands in the inbox you actually read.

**Steps**
1. **Settings → Email sender → Manage →**.
2. Check **Sender name**. It defaults to your business name.
3. Set **Reply-to email** to the inbox you check.
4. Add your **Mailing address**. The law requires it on marketing emails.
5. Set **Where should we send your alerts?** if you want enrops alerts somewhere other than your sign-in email.
6. Tap **Save**, then **Send test** to see an email the way families see it.

**Ennie's advice**
- If you leave reply-to blank, replies go to the email you signed up with. The page tells you which one it's using.
- Your emails are sent from an enrops address named after your page address, so you never have to touch domain settings. Families still see your name.

## Add a teammate {#team}

**Steps**
1. **Team → Invite teammate**.
2. Type their email and choose a role, then tap **Send invite**:
   - **Admin:** everything, including money and settings.
   - **Staff:** run programs, rosters and emails, but no money or settings.
   - **Viewer:** read-only, and can't see money.
3. They get a sign-in link by email.

## Things you can't change yourself yet {#not-yet-settings}

- **Time zone:** set from your browser when you signed up. To change it, email hello@enrops.com.
- **Business name:** email hello@enrops.com.
