# Questions providers ask

Each answer is short and points to the guide with the full steps. "Not yet" means it can't be done today.

## Getting started

### How do I sign in? {#faq-sign-in}
Go to enrops.com/login and use Google, or tap **Email me a sign-in link**. There's no password. See **Sign up**.

### Do I need a curriculum document to make a class? {#faq-curriculum}
No. Type a name, a short description and a price. See **Build your first class**.

### Can I change things after my class is live? {#faq-edit-live}
Yes. The name, price, times and dates can all be edited after you publish. Families who already registered keep the price they paid.

### Can I put registration on my own website? {#faq-embed}
Yes. On **Programs**, tap **Add to your website** and **Copy code**. Or link to your page, or print the QR code. See **Publish it and share your link**.

### Can families see only their own school's classes? {#faq-school-picker}
Yes. If your classes are at more than one location, families pick their school first, and only then see its classes and prices.

## Payments and fees

### Where does the money go, and when do I get it? {#faq-payouts}
Into your own Stripe account. Then Stripe pays your bank, about 2 business days after each payment on its standard US schedule. Your first payout can take longer. See **How you get paid**.

### What does enrops cost me? {#faq-cost}
enrops is free for businesses. Families pay a small enrops service fee with each registration, shown on its own line before they pay. The price screen shows exactly what families pay. Stripe's standard processing fee also applies, as with any card payment tool. See **What families pay**.

### Can I cover the fee myself instead of families? {#faq-cover-fee}
Yes. **Money → Payments → Who pays the enrops service fee?** Switch it from **Pass-through** to **Absorbed**. Families then pay only your price, and the fee comes out of your payout instead. This setting shows once Stripe is connected.

### Why are two prices different when I didn't set a discount? {#faq-two-prices}
It's usually the sibling discount. Check **Money → Discounts**. Leave the sibling box blank to turn it off.

### Does the fee apply to merchandise? {#faq-merch}
Merchandise doesn't run through enrops checkout yet. Keep selling it the way you do today.

### I stopped partway through Stripe. How do I get back in? {#faq-stripe-resume}
**Money → Payments → Continue setup**. It picks up where you left off. See **Connect Stripe**.

### Stripe wants a code or passkey I don't have. {#faq-stripe-passkey}
Look for another way to verify on that Stripe screen, such as a text message. If there isn't one, stop and email hello@enrops.com with what it says.

### Can I use the Stripe account I already have? {#faq-stripe-existing}
Yes. Tap **Connect my Stripe account**, not **I don't use Stripe yet**.

## Dates and terms

### My class dates include a school holiday. {#faq-holiday}
The location probably has no district, or the district has no calendar saved. See **Make your dates skip no-school days**.

### An early-release day was imported as no class. How do I add it back? {#faq-early-release}
**Programs → School calendar**, tap **Class times** on that calendar, and choose **Yes, I teach on early-release days**.

### How do I cancel just one session? {#faq-one-session}
Mark it as a no-school day: open the class and tap **Mark a no-school day**. Choose **Just this school**, or every class in that district skips the date too. The schedule runs a week longer and families see the new dates.

### What does FA26 or WI27 mean? {#faq-term-codes}
Season plus year: FA fall, WI winter, SP spring, SU summer. WI27 is winter 2027. Winter is December to February, so a class starting in December or January is in winter. A class goes in the season of its first class.

### How do I copy my classes to next term? {#faq-copy-term}
**Copy classes to another term** on Programs. Copies are drafts and need a first class date before you publish. See **Copy your classes to the next term**.

### Can I run a year-long class? {#faq-year-long}
Not yet as its own option. Email hello@enrops.com.

## Registration form and waivers

### How do I add my own question to the form? {#faq-custom-question}
**Settings → Registration questions → + Add question**. Pick **Short text** if you want words back, not a checkbox. See **Save your registration questions**.

### Where do I change what families see after they pay? {#faq-confirmation-page}
**Settings → Branding → Confirmation page wording**. You can add a button and a link there.

### Can the photo release be required? {#faq-photo-release}
Families can decline the photo release. A child without one on file shows as pending when you add them by hand.

### Who wrote my cancellation policy? {#faq-policy}
We did, so your page wasn't missing one. Read it and make it yours. See **Read your waivers and cancellation policy**.

## Families

### A parent's email bounced. How do I fix it? {#faq-fix-email}
**Comms → Contacts**, find the family, and tap **Edit**.

### How do parents get into the parent portal? {#faq-parent-portal}
They go to your page, tap sign in, and get a link by email. To nudge them, use **Invite families →** on the class roster. See **Invite families to the parent portal**.

### Can a second parent get their own login? {#faq-second-parent}
Not yet. Only the parent who registered can sign in. The second parent does get the messages you send from **Message families**.

### Can I add or remove a family contact by hand? {#faq-contact-add-remove}
Not yet from the Contacts screen. You can add a child to a roster by hand (see **Your rosters**).

### How do I see the waitlist? {#faq-waitlist}
Open the class with **View / print →**. The **Waitlist** section lists families in order. When a place opens, the next family in line is emailed and has 24 hours to take it. If they don't, it goes to the next family.

### Can I export my families or payments? {#faq-export}
You can download a class roster as a CSV from **View / print →**. Payments are in your Stripe dashboard. For anything else, email hello@enrops.com.

## Emails

### Did my email actually go out? {#faq-sent}
Emails sent from **Message families** are listed on its **Sent** tab.

### Can I test an email before sending it? {#faq-test-email}
Yes. Use **Send a test to** or **Email me a copy** in Message families, and **Send test** under **Settings → Email sender**.

### Why did a parent's reply go somewhere else? {#faq-reply-to}
Check **Settings → Email sender → Reply-to email**. If it's blank, replies go to the email you signed up with.

### Can I add a photo to an email to families? {#faq-email-photo}
Not yet.

### Can I text families or instructors? {#faq-sms}
Not yet.

### Why does "Message families" say fewer families than kids? {#faq-family-count}
It counts families, not children. Siblings share one email.

## Rosters and refunds

### What does "unfinished registration" mean? {#faq-unfinished}
A family started registering and didn't pay. They aren't on your roster yet.

### How do I refund a family? {#faq-refund}
**Programs → Class rosters**, expand the class, tap **Refund / remove**. See **Refund a family**.

### Does a class stop selling when it starts? {#faq-close}
Not when it starts. It comes off your page after its last session (see **When families see your classes**). To stop sign-ups once every class has started, turn off **Settings → Late sign-ups**. To stop one class sooner, **Unpublish** it. See **Stop selling a class**.

### What does a family pay if they join late? {#faq-late-join}
Only for the sessions left: the price × sessions left ÷ total sessions. A family joining an 8-session $275 class with 4 sessions left pays $137.50. Their card and checkout say "Started Nov 2 · 4 of 8 sessions left". Sibling discounts and promo codes still apply.

### Why does my page say "No open programs yet"? {#faq-no-programs}
Every published class has either finished, or started while **Settings → Late sign-ups** is off. Publish your next classes, or turn Late sign-ups back on.

### How do I send the roster to the school? {#faq-roster-partner}
**View / print → Email roster to partner**. See **Send the roster to your school partner**.

## Instructors (founding and enterprise plans)

### How do I add a teacher? {#faq-add-instructor}
**Instructors → Instructor Roster → + Add instructor**, then **Send onboarding invite**.

### A teacher with a school email keeps getting signed out. {#faq-school-email}
School and work email systems sometimes block sign-in links. A personal Gmail is the most reliable.

### Can I turn off the sub coordination email? {#faq-sub-email}
Not yet.

## Getting help

### Who do I ask? {#faq-help}
Tap **Share feedback** in the menu, or email hello@enrops.com. A real person on the enrops team answers.
